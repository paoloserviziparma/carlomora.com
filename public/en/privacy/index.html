Types of data processed and purposes of processing

Browsing data

The IT systems and software procedures used to operate this website acquire, in the course of their normal operation, certain personal data whose transmission is implicit in the use of Internet communication protocols.
This category includes the IP addresses or domain names of the computers and devices used by users, the URI/URL (Uniform Resource Identifier/Locator) addresses of the resources requested, the time of the request, the method used to submit the request to the server, the size of the file obtained in response, the numerical code indicating the status of the server's response (successful, error, etc.), some information on the geolocation of the request and other parameters relating to the user's operating system and IT environment (fingerprint).
This information is not collected in order to be associated with identified data subjects, but by its very nature it could allow users to be identified.
These data, which are necessary for the use of the web services, are also processed in order to obtain statistical information on the use of the services (most visited pages, number of visitors per hour or day, geographical areas of origin, etc.) and to check that the services offered are working correctly.
The data (log files, traffic data) may be used to establish liability in the event of possible computer crimes against the website, and for the same reason are kept by the ISP (Internet Service Provider) and by the web host.

Data provided by the user

The optional, explicit and voluntary sending of messages to the contact addresses shown on the website entails the acquisition of the sender's contact details (e-mail address) needed to reply, as well as of any personal data included in the messages. This processing does not require consent, as it is necessary for the performance of a contract or of pre-contractual measures at the data subject's request in order to reply to the requests received; providing the data is therefore necessary to deal with the request, and failure to provide them would make it impossible to follow up on it.
Sending messages does not entail automatic subscription to any promotional or commercial mailing list.
The data collected in this way are kept for the time strictly necessary to reply to the messages. If a business relationship is established, the data will be kept at least for the entire duration of that relationship.

Recipients of the data

Browsing data are kept by the ISP (Internet Service Provider) and by the web host (the entity hosting the web server on its own physical machines), which keep them for the periods required by law.
Personal data collected through messages and contacts are also processed by internal staff authorised by the controller, who act on the basis of specific instructions regarding the purposes and methods of processing.
No profiling is carried out and the data collected are not subject to automated decision-making.
The website is hosted on the Netlify platform (Netlify, Inc.), which acts as data processor for the hosting service and guarantees personal data protection standards compliant with the GDPR. Web servers may be located in different countries, both inside and outside the European Union (for example in the United States), and visitors' browsing data may therefore also be stored in those countries. Transfers of data outside the European Union are carried out ensuring an adequate level of protection, in accordance with the Standard Contractual Clauses approved by the European Commission.
