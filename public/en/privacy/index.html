Browsing data

The website is hosted by Netlify, Inc. Like any web server, it automatically logs some technical data about requests (IP address, date and time, page requested, browser and operating system, response code). The website has no contact forms. If you message us on WhatsApp, call us or email us, we process the data you give us (name, phone number or email address, message content) only to reply and manage your appointment. Legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to or under a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Data may be processed by Netlify, Inc. as processor for website hosting. Data are never sold, shared for marketing purposes or used for profiling.

Retention

Server logs are kept for the period set by the hosting service. Messages you send us are kept for as long as needed to reply and manage our relationship with you.

Your rights
