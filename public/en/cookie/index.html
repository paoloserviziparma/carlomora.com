Cookie policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

This website uses no cookies, neither technical nor profiling ones, and no other tracking tools such as pixels, localStorage or fingerprinting. That's why there is no banner to accept.

How we count visits

Visit statistics come from Netlify Analytics, which counts page views from the logs of the server hosting the website, without installing anything on your device. The data are aggregated and do not identify you. More details in the privacy policy.

Fonts, images and external content

Fonts and images are hosted on our own server: the website does not contact services such as Google Fonts. No videos, maps or social media posts are embedded.

External links

WhatsApp buttons and links to Instagram, Facebook and Maps open third-party websites or apps, whose own cookie policies apply.
