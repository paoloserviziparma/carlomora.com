What are cookies?

Cookies are small text strings that a website can send to and install on the user's device during browsing in order to store certain information about the user; this information is then sent back to the web server hosting the website on subsequent visits.

What are cookies used for?

Cookies are used for different purposes: to carry out computer authentication (for example to access a personal account), to monitor sessions and to preserve the user's settings when moving from one web page to another (for example remembering the display language of the website), to store information on specific configurations of users accessing the web server, to store browsing preferences, to collect anonymous statistical data (for example to monitor visits to the website), and for marketing.

Cookies can first be divided into two broad categories according to who sets them: first-party cookies, installed by the operator of the website the user is visiting, and third-party cookies, installed by other services (for example Google, Facebook, etc.).

As regards their duration, there are session cookies, which are automatically deleted when the browser is closed, and persistent cookies, which remain until their set expiry date.

Finally, cookies can be divided into technical or necessary cookies, needed to browse the website and provide the service, for example to carry out authentication, remember the language in which the website is displayed, etc.; analytics or statistical cookies, used by website operators to collect information on the number of users and how they visit the website, and thus to produce statistics on the service and its use; and profiling or marketing cookies, used to attribute specific actions or recurring behaviour patterns in the use of the service's features to specific, identified or identifiable individuals, in order to create profiles grouped into homogeneous clusters so that targeted advertising messages can be sent, i.e. in line with the preferences expressed by the user while browsing.

Technical cookies do not require the user's consent to be installed and used, and are therefore also referred to as necessary, whereas profiling cookies, both first- and third-party, may only be used with the user's prior consent; finally, analytics or statistical cookies are treated like technical cookies only if they collect anonymous data, otherwise they are also subject to the user's prior consent.

What type of cookies does this website use?

This website uses technical cookies, needed to ensure an adequate level of security (for example by preventing Cross-Site Scripting attacks and distinguishing human users from bots), to remember the user's login during the session so they do not have to log in again on every page, to remember the user's display language and to store the consent given through the pop-up banner on the home page.

There are also statistical cookies, whose purpose is to record users' page views so as to provide us with up-to-date statistics on the use of the services offered. By clicking the “Reject all” button on the pop-up banner on the home page, these cookies will not be installed, since prior consent to their installation is required.

Retention period

Technical cookies are kept on the user's device for different periods: some are deleted when the browsing session ends (so-called session cookies), while others are kept for periods ranging from 30 minutes to 2 years.

Statistical cookies are kept for periods ranging from 1 day to two years.

Disabling cookies in the browser

Each user can configure their browser so that it does not automatically accept cookies. It is possible to delete all cookies installed in the browser or to block the installation of new ones. For specific instructions for each type of browser, select one of the following links:

  • SAFARI: https://support.apple.com/kb/PH19214?viewlocale=it_IT&locale=en_US
  • CHROME: https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/61416?hl=en
  • FIREFOX: https://support.mozilla.org/en-US/kb/enable-and-disable-cookies-website-preferences
  • OPERA: http://help.opera.com/Windows/10.00/en/cookies.html

Browser settings also allow you to delete cookies and browsing data (each browser has its own method, so follow the relevant guides available online); by doing so and returning to the website, the banner will be shown again and you will be able to renew or give your consent again. Remember that this operation may delete other data saved in the browser, including data relating to other websites (for example passwords), so select only the functions for deleting cookies and browsing data (for example the browser history and cache).

Finally, you can enable the “Do Not Track” option available in most modern browsers. Websites designed to respect this option should automatically stop collecting some of your browsing data when it is enabled. As mentioned, however, not all websites are set up to respect this option.

However, completely disabling cookies is not recommended, as it could make browsing impossible; it is advisable to choose a setting that disables tracking elements only.
