01 — Our philosophy

Yes, we're rock.

“I know what I'd like you to find when you walk into our salon. Let's see if I get it right.”

It has to be a place full of energy, design, art, colour, recycling, originality and irony; a place with answers to what you need, whether you have little time for your hair or simply don't feel like spending any.

Being stylish means finding empathy, changing your look without answering to anyone, always feeling put together and, above all, yourself. We're here for those who love detail and have the courage to break the mould.

We prefer imperfection#, for those who feel at ease being imperfect. That's why the hashtag goes at the end.

06 — FAQ

Good to know

How do I book an appointment?

The quickest way is to message us on WhatsApp at +39 391 1156147: tell us which day you prefer and the service you'd like, and we'll reply with availability. You can also call us on +39 0521 285916 during opening hours.

Where is the salon?

We are at Viale Mentana 12/a, 43121 Parma (PR), Italy.

Are you open on Sundays and Mondays?

No, the salon is closed on Sundays and Mondays. We are open Tuesday to Saturday: Tuesday and Thursday 9:00–18:00, Wednesday 12:00–20:00, Friday 10:00–20:00, Saturday 9:00–18:00.

Do the opening hours change in summer?

Yes: in July and August we are open on Wednesdays from 10:00 to 20:00 and on Saturdays from 9:00 to 15:00.

What is Art Hair Studios by Wella?

It's a network of hair salons founded in 1976 and linked to Wella, presenting a trend collection every season. Carlo Mora is part of the network.
